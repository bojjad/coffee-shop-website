Our story

About Brew & Bean

Brew & Bean began as a small corner shop with one espresso machine and a simple idea: coffee should feel personal. We still roast in small batches, greet regulars by name, and keep the lights warm enough for lingering over a book.

Our beans come from partner farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. We bake pastries in-house, brew with filtered water, and believe a good café is part kitchen, part living room.

  • Small-batch roasting twice a week
  • House-baked pastries every morning
  • Community tables, quiet corners, and free Wi-Fi
